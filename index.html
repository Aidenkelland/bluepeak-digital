Core service 02

AI Automation

Practical AI for the enquiries you already receive, with a person in control of what goes out.

Enquiry management

Keep every enquiry in one place, track its status from new to won or lost, and see a full activity history.

AI-assisted classification

Identify the service requested, summarise the enquiry and list any details that are missing.

Urgency detection

Rank enquiries as emergency, high or normal, and flag anything a person should check first.

Response drafting

Prepare a reply and check it for commitments nobody has confirmed, such as callbacks, appointments or prices.

Follow-up management

Create a follow-up task with a due time based on urgency, so enquiries stay visible until they are dealt with.

Human approval

AI drafts. People decide.

LeadFlow drafts replies but never sends them automatically. Nothing reaches a customer until a person has reviewed it, approved it and sent it themselves.
